## ✅ YES, You Can Embed Your Facebook Videos!

Any **public** video posted on your Facebook profile can be embedded on your watch website.

---

## 🎯 Quick Steps (60 Seconds)

### Desktop Method (Easiest):

```
1. Go to facebook.com in your browser
2. Navigate to your profile (facebook.com/sherard.ng)
3. Find the watch video you want to embed
4. Right-click on the video
5. Select "Copy video URL" from the menu
6. Go to your admin dashboard
7. Add/Edit a watch
8. Scroll to "Product Video" section
9. Click the "Facebook" button
10. Paste the URL
11. Video preview appears automatically
12. Save the watch
13. Done! ✅
```

### Mobile Method:

```
1. Open Facebook app
2. Go to your profile
3. Find the watch video
4. Tap on the video to open it
5. Tap the Share button (arrow icon)
6. Select "Copy Link"
7. Open your admin dashboard on mobile/tablet
8. Add/Edit a watch
9. Scroll to "Product Video"
10. Select "Facebook"
11. Paste the link
12. Save
13. Done! ✅
```

## 📱 What the URLs Look Like

### Valid Facebook Video URLs:

```
✅ From profile video:
https://www.facebook.com/sherard.ng/videos/123456789012345

✅ From post:
https://www.facebook.com/sherard.ng/posts/123456789

✅ From watch link:
https://fb.watch/abc123def

✅ From reel (if public):
https://www.facebook.com/reel/123456789
```

### What Gets Converted:

When you paste a Facebook URL, the system automatically converts it to:

```
https://www.facebook.com/plugins/video.php?href=[your-video]&show_text=false&autoplay=1
```

This is the **embed format** that makes videos play on your website.

## ⚠️ Important Requirements

### Video MUST Be:

1. **PUBLIC** ✅
   - Not "Friends Only"
   - Not "Only Me"
   - Not in a private group
   - Check privacy icon when posting

2. **On Your Profile/Page** ✅
   - Posted by Sherard W Ng
   - Or posted on Manila Watch Atelier page (if you have one)
   - Or shared publicly to your timeline

3. **Not Deleted** ✅
   - Video still exists on Facebook
   - Not archived or hidden

### How to Check if Video is Public:

```
Desktop:
1. Open the video post
2. Look for the globe icon (🌐) near the timestamp
3. Globe = Public ✅
4. Two people icon = Friends Only ❌
5. Lock icon = Private ❌

Mobile:
1. Open the video
2. Tap the three dots (...)
3. See "Edit Privacy"
4. Should show "Public" ✅
```

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Problem: "Video URL doesn't work"

**Solution:**
1. Check if video is still public
2. Try copying URL again
3. Test the URL by opening it in incognito/private browser
4. Make sure you copied the full URL
5. Try a different video to isolate the issue

### Problem: "Video shows but won't play"

**Solution:**
1. Video might be private - check privacy settings
2. Video might be deleted - verify it's still on Facebook
3. Geographic restrictions - some videos have location limits
4. Ad blockers - try disabling browser extensions

### Problem: "Can't find 'Copy video URL' option"

**Solution:**
1. You might be on mobile - use the Share button instead
2. Right-click directly on the video player
3. If still not there, click the timestamp to open video in new page, then copy browser URL

### Problem: "Video embed shows 'Content Not Available'"

**Solution:**
1. Video was made private or deleted on Facebook
2. Log into Facebook and check if you can still see it
3. Check video privacy settings
4. Re-upload video as public if needed
