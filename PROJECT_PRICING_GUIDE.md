## 💡 Negotiation Tips

### If client asks for lower price:
1. **Show the hours**: 42 hours at even ₱1,000/hr = ₱42k minimum
2. **Compare to alternatives**: Hiring agency would be ₱200k-300k
3. **Highlight value**: Managing ₱50M inventory, saves countless hours
4. **Offer payment plan**: 50% upfront, 50% on delivery
5. **Remove features**: Remove documentation, support, or maintenance

### If client accepts higher price:
1. **Add value**: Throw in extra support month or training
2. **Build relationship**: "First project discount, but ongoing work at full rate"
3. **Set precedent**: For future enhancements, quote properly
